Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

What data is used

When the account owner connects a platform such as TikTok, Autopost receives an access token and basic profile information (for example, the display name). It also handles the video files and captions that the owner chooses to publish.

How it is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos to his own accounts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and files are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Autopost has no public servers or user database.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services) and delete the local files.
